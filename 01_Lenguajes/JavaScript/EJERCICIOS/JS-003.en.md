# JS-003 — Operators and total calculations

- **Topic:** arithmetic operations and calculation order.
- **Level:** beginner.
- **Requirements:** JS-001 and JS-002.
- **Type:** practical exercise.

## Objective

Use multiplication, percentages, and subtraction to calculate a purchase total.

## Task

Calculate a purchase using a unit price, quantity, and discount. Display the subtotal, savings, and final total with two decimal places.

## Requirements

- [ ] Calculate the subtotal by multiplying price by quantity.
- [ ] Calculate the discount by multiplying the subtotal by the percentage.
- [ ] Subtract the discount from the subtotal.
- [ ] Display all three results with toFixed(2).
- [ ] Do not manually type the final results.

## Starter file

Complete [ejercicio.js](JS-003/ejercicio.js).

## Expected result

With the sample values, the subtotal is 269.70, savings are 26.97, and the total is 242.73.

## Check your work

Change the quantity or discount and confirm that all results are recalculated.
