# JS-004 — Conditionals and logical operators

- **Topic:** if, else, comparison, and logical OR.
- **Level:** beginner.
- **Requirements:** JS-001 to JS-003.
- **Type:** practical exercise.

## Objective

Make a decision using a condition and combine two criteria with the logical OR (||) operator.

## Task

A store offers free shipping when a purchase reaches the minimum amount or when the customer belongs to its frequent-customer program. Otherwise, shipping costs 80.

## Requirements

- [ ] Keep the purchase, minimum purchase, and customer-type values.
- [ ] Use if and else to choose the shipping cost.
- [ ] Combine the conditions with ||.
- [ ] Display whether shipping is free or has a cost.
- [ ] Display the shipping cost with two decimal places.

## Starter file

Complete [ejercicio.js](JS-004/ejercicio.js).

## Expected result

With the sample values, shipping is free because the customer is a frequent customer, even though the purchase does not reach the minimum.

## Check your work

Test these cases: frequent customer with a small purchase, non-frequent customer with a large purchase, and non-frequent customer with a small purchase.
