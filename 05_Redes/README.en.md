# Networking

Fundamentals of communication between computers, applications, and services.

## Planned contents

- Network concepts and models.
- IP addresses, subnets, and ports.
- TCP and UDP.
- DNS.
- HTTP and HTTPS.
- Requests, responses, and status codes.
- Client, server, and architecture.
- Diagnostic tools in authorized environments.

## Responsible use

Practice is limited to networks, devices, and services owned by the learner or used with permission. The focus is understanding protocols, diagnosing connectivity, and analyzing lab traffic.
